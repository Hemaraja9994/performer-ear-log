Protect your hearing and your music

  • Musician's earplugs lower the volume evenly, so the sound stays natural. Custom-moulded ones fit best.
  • Stand beside or behind loud drums and speakers, not directly in front of them.
  • Rest your ears after a long performance. Quiet time helps the ears recover.
  • Get a hearing test once a year, and sooner if ringing or muffled hearing does not clear within a day.
